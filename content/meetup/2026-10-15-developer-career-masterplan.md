### Developer Career Masterplan

Looking for a plan to take your career to senior levels and beyond, and help you reach an outstanding reputation and the best positions in the market?
From getting the first job to getting the best job, from technology to creating amazing impact in the world, this interactive career conversation will both offer a proven masterplan, but Bruno will also discuss real career issues that you may have.

Based on the book of the same name by Bruno Souza and Heather VanCura, this presentation will walk you through the most important steps of technical career advancement, and will help you build your personal plan for an expert career.

#### 🎤 Speaker: Bruno Souza

Since 1995, Bruno has been helping Java developers advance their careers and work on exciting projects. A recognized Java Evangelist and Java Champion, as well as a board member of the Java Community Process, Bruno founded and leads SouJava, the Brazilian Java Users Society. In his book, "Developer Career Masterplan," Bruno shares insights on career development for senior developers, topics he further explores in his Code4.life project.
