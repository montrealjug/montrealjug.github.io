### Netty in the real world

Netty is a mature, high-performance, asynchronous event-driven network application framework used at the heart of countless JVM projects. In this session, Sean Sullivan shares practical, real-world experience building and operating systems on top of Netty.

#### 🎤 Speaker: Sean Sullivan

Sean Sullivan is a software engineer passionate about Java, distributed systems, and open source software, and an organizer of the Portland Java User Group. His areas of focus include Java, Apache Iceberg, and Netty, and he contributes to many widely used open source projects.

📍 Lieu : Montréal, QC
